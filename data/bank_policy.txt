# Loan Approval Agent - Bank Policy Document

## Credit Score Requirements
- Minimum credit score required for loan approval: 650
- Credit scores between 650-700: May be approved with higher interest rates
- Credit scores above 750: Eligible for premium loan rates and higher loan amounts
- Credit scores below 600: Generally not eligible for unsecured loans

## Income Requirements
- Minimum monthly income required: $2,500
- Debt-to-income ratio should not exceed 43%
- Co-applicant income is considered for joint applications
- Self-employed applicants must provide 2 years of income documentation
- Salaried employees from government or reputed companies are preferred

## Employment Status
- Minimum employment tenure: 6 months at current employer
- Self-employed must have business running for at least 2 years
- Unemployed applicants are not eligible for personal loans
- Part-time workers must show consistent income for 12 months

## Loan Amount Limits
- Minimum loan amount: $1,000
- Maximum loan amount: 10 times the monthly income
- First-time borrowers limited to $50,000 maximum
- Loan term options: 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months

## Property Area Considerations
- Urban properties: Standard evaluation
- Semi-urban properties: May require additional documentation
- Rural properties: Subject to additional verification and may have lower approval rates

## Marital Status and Dependents
- Joint applications with spouse may increase loan eligibility
- Number of dependents affects disposable income calculation

## Loan Purpose Categories
- Home loans: Lowest interest rates, highest loan amounts
- Education loans: Special schemes available with grace period
- Personal loans: Standard interest rates apply
- Business loans: Require business plan and collateral for amounts above $25,000

## Documentation Requirements
- Valid government-issued ID
- Proof of income (salary slips or tax returns)
- Bank statements for the last 6 months
- Address proof
- Employment verification letter
- Credit report authorization

## Common Reasons for Loan Rejection
1. Credit score below minimum threshold
2. Insufficient income to support loan EMI
3. High existing debt obligations
4. Unstable employment history
5. Incomplete or fraudulent documentation
6. Previous loan defaults or delinquencies
7. Too many recent credit inquiries

## Tips to Improve Loan Approval Chances
1. Maintain a credit score above 700
2. Pay off existing debts before applying
3. Ensure stable employment for at least 1 year
4. Keep bank balance healthy
5. Apply for loan amount within your eligibility
6. Provide accurate and complete documentation
7. Consider adding a co-applicant with good credit

## Interest Rate Structure
- Base rate: 8.5% per annum
- Credit score above 750: Base rate + 0%
- Credit score 700-750: Base rate + 1%
- Credit score 650-700: Base rate + 2.5%
- Processing fee: 1-2% of loan amount
- Prepayment penalty: None for floating rate loans

## Eligibility Criteria Summary
To be eligible for a loan, applicants must:
- Be between 21 and 65 years of age
- Have a minimum credit score of 650
- Have a stable source of income
- Meet the minimum income requirements
- Have a debt-to-income ratio below 43%
- Provide all required documentation
